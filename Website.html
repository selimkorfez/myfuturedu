About My Future Education

Teaching Turkish with Heart

My Future Education was founded by Sevgi Körfez, a passionate educator and mother based in Leicester who believes that language is the gateway to a child's cultural identity.

For Turkish families living in the UK, keeping the mother tongue alive is both a challenge and a gift. Sevgi's mission is to help children maintain their connection to Turkish language, culture and heritage — with joy, structure and love.
