1. Overview

This policy explains how My Future Education uses cookies and similar storage/access technologies on myfuturedu.com.

At the time of this policy, the public website is designed to operate without advertising trackers. A small item may be stored in your browser's local storage to remember your cookie choice. This helps us avoid repeatedly showing the privacy choices notice.

2. Essential technologies

Technologies that are strictly necessary to provide a service you request, maintain security or remember a necessary preference may be used without consent where the law allows. We will still explain their purpose where appropriate.

3. Optional analytics or other technologies

If we introduce optional analytics, embedded media, advertising or other non-essential technologies, we will review whether consent is required before they are activated. Where consent is required, they will remain off until a user makes a valid choice.

4. Your choices

You can use the cookie/privacy controls shown on the website and can also clear browser storage through your browser settings. Withdrawing an optional choice will not affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred before withdrawal.

5. Third-party services

External services used for lessons or payments may operate under their own cookie and privacy policies when you visit or use those services. We will link to or identify them where appropriate when those services are introduced.

6. Changes

We will update this page when our website technologies change. If a material change requires a fresh consent choice, we will ask again.
