Privacy Policy

How My Future Education handles personal information relating to parents, carers and learners when you enquire about or use our Turkish language tuition services.

Last updated: 30 August 2026

1. Who we are

My Future Education provides Turkish language tuition for children and young people, online across the UK and in person in Leicester, England. For data protection purposes, My Future Education is the controller of the personal information described in this notice.

Privacy contact: info@myfuturedu.com.

Our enquiry and booking forms are intended to be completed by a parent or carer. Children under 13 should not submit personal information to us directly without a parent or carer.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you contact us or use our services, we may collect:

  • parent or carer name, email address, telephone number and correspondence;
  • the learner's first name, age or school year, language level, learning goals and lesson preferences;
  • booking, attendance, lesson and progress information;
  • payment and transaction records where paid services are used (card details should be handled by the payment provider rather than stored by us);
  • information you choose to provide about educational needs or reasonable adjustments;
  • technical information needed to keep the website secure and working; and
  • records relating to safeguarding concerns where this is necessary to protect a child.

Please avoid sending sensitive information unless it is genuinely needed for us to teach safely or make an appropriate adjustment.

3. Why we use personal information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, arrange and deliver lessons, communicate with parents or carers, tailor learning, manage payments and records, deal with complaints, keep children safe, prevent misuse and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

Our lawful bases may include taking steps at your request before entering a contract, performing a contract, complying with a legal obligation, our legitimate interests in running a safe and effective tuition service, and consent where the law requires it.

4. Children's information

Because our services concern children, we take particular care with their information. We aim to collect only what is reasonably needed to teach, communicate with the parent or carer, support progress and safeguard the learner.

We do not use children's information for behavioural advertising, sell children's personal information, or intentionally profile children for advertising. Parent or carer contact details should be used for routine account, booking and support communications.

5. Who we share information with

We may use trusted providers that help us operate the service, such as website hosting, email, video-conferencing, payment, accounting or administrative providers. They should only receive information needed for the relevant purpose and be subject to appropriate contractual or legal protections.

We may also disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect a child or another person from harm. We do not sell personal information.

6. International transfers

Some technology providers may process information outside the UK. Where this happens, we will use an appropriate UK data-transfer mechanism or other lawful safeguard where required.

7. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected for, including teaching, safeguarding, accounting, legal and dispute-resolution needs. Enquiries that do not lead to lessons will normally be deleted or anonymised when no longer needed. Financial records are retained for the periods required by applicable UK tax and accounting rules. Safeguarding records may need to be retained for longer where this is necessary and lawful.

8. Security

We use reasonable organisational and technical measures to protect information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so please do not send unnecessary sensitive information by ordinary email.

9. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to ask for access to personal information, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent. Rights are subject to legal conditions and exemptions.

To make a data protection request or complaint, email info@myfuturedu.com. We will take reasonable steps to verify identity before disclosing personal information.

If you remain unhappy with how your personal information has been handled, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

10. Marketing

We will not send optional electronic marketing where consent is required unless the appropriate permission has been obtained. You can unsubscribe from optional marketing at any time.

11. Cookies and similar technologies

See our Cookie Policy for information about storage and access technologies used by the website and how preferences are managed.

12. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our services, providers or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this website with an updated date.

13. Legal framework

This notice is intended to reflect applicable UK data protection and privacy law, including the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018 and PECR as amended, including relevant changes made by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025.
